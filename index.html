Type an address up to the neighborhood (e.g. 서울 강남구 역삼동) in Origin and Destination and pick a suggestion. It works without any external service. Seoul neighborhoods are built in; elsewhere the district or city center is used. The detail field takes building, floor, or room. Then choose how you travel: the comparison shows the carbon for every mode, and clicking one applies it. The road distance is filled in automatically and you can still edit it. Use the detail field for building, floor, or room. If you press Enter or click away without picking, the address is used as typed. With a Kakao key set, results come from Kakao Maps.
